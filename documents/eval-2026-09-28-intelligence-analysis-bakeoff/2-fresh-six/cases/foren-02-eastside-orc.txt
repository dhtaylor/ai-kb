THREAT ASSESSMENT: Eastside Organized Retail Crime Network

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: The Eastside retail crime network has likely ceased operations following the August arrests. No additional incidents have been reported in the past six weeks. The network no longer represents an active threat to district retailers.

ANALYSIS
Confidential informant CI-7, an embedded member of the network, reported on 22 August that the primary organizer ('Ramos') fled the jurisdiction following the arrest of three associates on 14 August. CI-7 stated that remaining members were 'spooked' and had suspended activities indefinitely. No commercial burglaries matching the network's known signature methods — glass-cut entry, cargo-van staging — have been recorded in the Eastside district in the six weeks since the arrests.

The six-week absence of signature incidents, combined with CI-7's reporting, confirms that the network is dormant and no longer poses an operational threat. Protective security resources deployed to district retailers may be stood down.
