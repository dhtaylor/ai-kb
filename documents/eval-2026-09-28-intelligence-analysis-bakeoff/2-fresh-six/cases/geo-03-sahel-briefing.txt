INTELLIGENCE BRIEFING — MAY 2025
SUBJECT: Africa Corps Expansion and Sahel Junta Cohesion

BACKGROUND: Following the 2021–2023 coups in Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger, the Alliance of Sahel States (AES) expelled French forces and formalized security partnerships with Russia's Africa Corps (formerly Wagner Group). Estimated Africa Corps presence: Mali 1,000–1,500 personnel; Burkina Faso 100–300; Niger figures unconfirmed.

ASSESSMENT: The AES bloc is cohesive and ideologically aligned against Western influence. This trajectory will very likely result in Africa Corps establishing a permanent forward logistics hub in Mali within 12 months, after which Africa Corps will expand operations into Niger and subsequently provide Russia a strategic platform to contest both French and US access across the entire Sahel corridor.

No significant ECOWAS pressure or credible external intervention has materialized to reverse junta consolidation, which confirms that AES governments have achieved durable domestic stability.

COLLECTION BASIS: Commercial imagery, open-source reporting, diplomatic cable traffic.
