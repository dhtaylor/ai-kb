## IA Review — Scout's note: Lillard clutch performance

**Reviewed at:** Light level — a three-sentence offhand scout's note, not a formal or externally-read product; full-rubric review would be disproportionate.
**Analytic line (BLUF as written):** "Most clutch player in the league, no question."

### Strengths
- The claim is grounded in a concrete, checkable data point rather than pure assertion — "Lillard dropped four buzzer-beaters this postseason" gives the reader something to verify, which is better than an unsupported vibe (partial credit toward Standard #1).
- The bottom line is short and unambiguous — there's no hunting for what the note is actually claiming (Standard #6).

### Weaknesses (most to least threatening to the conclusion)
1. **Law of small numbers** — Cognitive bias (oversensitivity to consistency / law of small numbers), bearing on Standards #8 (accurate judgments) and #3 (information vs. judgment)
   > "Lillard dropped four buzzer-beaters this postseason. The numbers don't lie — he simply doesn't feel pressure. Most clutch player in the league, no question."
   Why it matters: four made shots in one postseason (no attempts, misses, or career baseline given) is a short streak being read as a fixed psychological trait and then extrapolated into a superlative, no-hedge claim about the entire league — exactly the pattern where a small, favorable sample feels like strong evidence but isn't. "The numbers don't lie" also asserts the interpretation as settled fact rather than a judgment drawn from four data points.
   Fix: Soften to what the sample supports — e.g., "Lillard hit four clutch shots this postseason, which is a strong small sample but not enough on its own to call him the league's most clutch player; that claim needs a look at attempts/misses too, and across more than one postseason."

### Scorecard (only applicable standards)

### If I were the analyst, the one change I'd make first
Drop "no question" and reframe the claim as what four made shots actually support — a strong data point, not league-wide certainty.
