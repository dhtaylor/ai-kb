SOLARIS ENERGY (SLRE) — EQUITY INITIATION

Solaris Energy is a Texas-based distributed-solar developer that IPO'd in March 2024 at $14/share and currently trades at $9.40. The company operates 1.2 GW of installed commercial and industrial rooftop capacity and carries a 3.8 GW development pipeline.

MARKET POSITION
SLRE benefits from IRA tax-credit tailwinds, a deep pipeline of signed letters of intent, and a management team with prior successful exits in renewables. Commercial solar is growing at approximately 22% annually per Wood Mackenzie's Q3 2024 market report.

FINANCIAL OUTLOOK
We model FY2026 revenue of $310M (up from $185M in FY2024), contingent on: (a) IRA production tax credits surviving the next congressional cycle intact; (b) SLRE converting 60%+ of its LOI pipeline to signed power-purchase agreements; and (c) management executing a 2.1× capacity expansion without material cost overruns. Assuming all three conditions hold, EBITDA margins reach 24% and the stock re-rates to 12× EV/EBITDA, implying a price target of $21 — representing 123% upside from current levels.

RISKS
Supply-chain delays and interest-rate sensitivity are the primary risks. IRA policy uncertainty is noted but viewed as low probability.

RECOMMENDATION
We initiate coverage with a BUY rating and a $21 price target.
