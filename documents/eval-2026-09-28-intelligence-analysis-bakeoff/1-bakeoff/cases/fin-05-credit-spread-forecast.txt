MACRO CREDIT OUTLOOK — Q3 2025 UPDATE

FORECAST
We revise our year-end investment-grade spread forecast to 95 bps, modestly above our January 2025 call of 88 bps. The revision reflects the tariff-driven widening in April (which briefly touched 96 bps) and emerging stress in commercial real estate, but does not materially alter our constructive view on corporate credit.

LEADING INDICATOR SIGNAL
Our primary leading indicator is the 6-month change in the Conference Board Leading Economic Index. Over the past 18 months, every 0.5-point monthly decline in the LEI has been followed within 60 days by an 8–12 bps widening in IG spreads. The LEI fell 1.0 point in April 2025 — the largest single-month drop since March 2023. We therefore forecast 8–12 bps of further widening into Q3. This relationship has held consistently, and we have high confidence in the directional call.

INFLATION LINKAGE
Core PCE and IG spreads have moved in tandem over the past 24 months: when PCE accelerates, spreads tighten. This pattern suggests that inflation is actually supportive of credit quality. We expect PCE to remain at 2.6–2.8%, which further underpins our constructive outlook.

RISKS
A sharper-than-expected consumer slowdown could push spreads well beyond our forecast; we assign roughly 10% probability to that tail.

CONCLUSION
We maintain a constructive view on IG credit, with year-end spreads in the 90–100 bps range.
