## IA Review — fin-01-fed-slack (Slack post to @portfolio-risk)

**Reviewed at:** Light level — a one-sentence chat post. A short message is Light whatever it concerns. The stakes (a portfolio rotation) do not promote it.
**Analytic line (bottom line as written):** "Tech multiples will compress and small-caps will underperform large-caps through year-end. Rotating: +5% TLT, −10% QQQ."

### Strengths
- The main message is up front and the post is short. The trade (+5% TLT, −10% QQQ) is stated plainly. This meets ICD 203 tradecraft standard 6, "present a clear main analytic message up front".
- The reported fact ("Powell just confirmed no cuts before December") is kept in its own sentence, apart from the forecast sentences that follow. This supports standard 3, distinguishing underlying information from assumptions and judgments.

### Weaknesses (most to least threatening to the conclusion)
1. **Forecast stated as certainty** — Standard 2, "Properly expresses and explains uncertainties associated with major analytic judgments", and standard 8, "express judgments as clearly and precisely as possible".
   > "Tech multiples will compress and small-caps will underperform large-caps through year-end."
   Why it matters: a confirmed Fed hold is information. Multiple compression and small-cap underperformance are judgments drawn from it. The post gives both as flat certainty, so the size of the rotation looks better supported than it is.
   Fix: put the judgment in a likelihood term from the library's scale, for example "Tech multiples are likely to compress and small-caps are likely to underperform large-caps through year-end." Per the library, "likely" is 55–80%. If you mean something stronger, "very likely" is 80–95%.

(One finding, at the Light cap. I did not flag missing alternatives, sourcing or customer relevance. The library's proportionality rule and this skill's Light-level checks both exclude them for a chat note.)

Overall read: a sound, quick, actionable note whose only real weakness is that its forecast is stated as fact. The fix is one word.

### If I were the analyst, the one change I'd make first
Replace "will" with a likelihood term: "Tech multiples are likely to compress and small-caps are likely to underperform large-caps through year-end."
