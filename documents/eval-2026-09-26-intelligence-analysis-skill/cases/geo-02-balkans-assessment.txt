SERIAL: EUR-2025-0441
SUBJECT: Serbian Force Posture and Intentions Regarding Kosovo, Q1 2025

KEY JUDGMENT: Serbia is unlikely to initiate military action against Kosovo in the next 90 days.

DISCUSSION:
Serbian armed forces conducted three combined-arms exercises near the Kosovo administrative boundary line (ABL) in January–February 2025, involving 1st Army armored elements and Air Force assets at Niš airbase. A senior official in Belgrade, quoted by state news agency Tanjug on 22 February, stated that the exercises were 'defensive in nature and consistent with NATO-standard readiness cycles.' Serbia has assessed these exercises as routine annual training rather than offensive preparations.

Belgrade's political calendar further supports the assessment. Parliamentary coalition negotiations remain ongoing, and President Vučić has staked significant personal capital on EU candidacy. A leader in Vučić's position would not risk jeopardizing EU accession — Belgrade's primary strategic priority — for a military gamble with uncertain outcomes.

CONFIDENCE: High.
