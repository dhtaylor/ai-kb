## IA Review — fin-01-fed-slack

**Reviewed at:** Light level — a Slack message to a `@portfolio-risk` channel: a quick, offhand market call and trade instruction, not a formal assessment.
**Analytic line (bottom line as written):** "Tech multiples will compress and small-caps will underperform large-caps through year-end" — driving the action "Rotating: +5% TLT, −10% QQQ."

### Strengths
- Reported input, the analyst's own call, and the resulting action are kept legibly separate — "Powell just confirmed no cuts before December" (reported), "Tech multiples will compress and small-caps will underperform..." (judgment), "Rotating: +5% TLT, −10% QQQ" (action) — satisfies Standard #3, properly distinguishing underlying information from the analyst's judgment.
- The action itself is unambiguous and executable — a specific instrument and size, not a vague "trim tech exposure" — satisfies Standard #6's requirement for a clear main message.

### Weaknesses (most to least threatening to the conclusion)
1. **Certainty outruns the basis** — Standard #2 (Properly expresses and explains uncertainties) / Standard #8 (Makes accurate judgments and assessments — confidence aligned to evidence quality, not overclaimed)
   > "Tech multiples will compress and small-caps will underperform large-caps through year-end."
   Why it matters: this is the single load-bearing claim the whole trade rests on, and it's stated as flat, certain fact ("will... will...") after two unstated inferential steps from one Fed data point (rate-cut timing → multiple compression → small-cap relative underperformance), with no calibrated likelihood or confidence attached to either step. This is the "Best-guess / uncertainty-drop" pattern (cognitive-biases-and-mitigations.md): treating a likely-but-uncertain input as if it were already 100% true.
   Fix: Swap "will compress" / "will underperform" for a calibrated likelihood term with a range (e.g., "likely, ~60–70%, to compress") per the estimative-probability ladder, rather than stating a two-step market forecast as a certainty.

### If I were the analyst, the one change I'd make first
Attach a calibrated likelihood (with a range) to "Tech multiples will compress and small-caps will underperform large-caps through year-end" instead of stating it as certain.
